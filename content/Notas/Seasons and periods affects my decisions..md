# Seasons and periods affects my decisions.
At the place where I live, geographically we're experiencing the ENSO (El Niño South Oscillation). It have a lot of detectable consequences thorought the year, and it's a global phenomena, but obviosly you won't notice it in the same way in every country. Maybe right now at Canadá there are not mentionable effects. 

Anyway, the point is that this climatological event had us feeling the air dry yesterday and fully wet today. The changes in weather are extreme. This morning the sun rised up at 6.40 am and stand still until now, that suddendly the day turn into a cloudy one. It started raining at 4 pm and now everything still awash. 

And my body can't get used to those drastical changes. I've been sleeping so bad recently. I get up late night every day studying, I lost the rythim of it a bit. Tomorrow I have an importanr exam, and now I'm feeling exhausted. I should have dancing classes but the rain demotivate us all so it got canceled. So now I don't know what to do with my time.

Is great to sometimes don't knowing what to do with your time. But I'll probably waste it if I don't know how to spend it. Tiredness usually makes me take bad decisions.
