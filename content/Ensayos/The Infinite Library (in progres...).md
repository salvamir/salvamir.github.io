## The Internet,<span class="tc-color tc-color--cyan" style="color: var(--color-cyan)"> the infinitely useless human's library.</span>
That's why we tend to think that the internet is useless. You've probably already heard of the Death Internet Theory. We refer to "alive" content not only to "human and personal" content, but for informationaly useful content. Forums, Wiki's and blogs fell apart because of money, AI, and us. Yep, it is our fault too... 

The internet used to be a limited place where real people expressed their experience and their learnings for free. Or at least that's the utopia that other people describes. I've never lived that era. 

Now, the internet isn't that useful anymore, and we need to find a solution to all of this. If we want to make it useful again, or at least, manage a way to surf thorough the garbage and stumble by quality information. 
#### Quality content and the quantity of it.
Which content is catalogued as "quality content" and which isn't? Also, we should distinguish the topic of that "content" too. Probably, quality content for gardeners won't be the same as for developers. In addition to that, not every topic have the same quantity of content. The mainstream era has began years ago, and now almost every easy-to-find website is content-seller oriented. 

It is undeniable that most of the accesable content online is awful and worthless. Many pieces of the "library" we thought the internet was, started to fall apart years ago. 

Some of the causes for why the content is low quality and high quantity now might be:
1. **Earning money throught the internet has become much easier:** Many sites started to monetize their published stuff. So now, most of the "useful content" is behind a pay-wall. And the "human content" became less transparent and sincere. Only if you can find it in the huge sea of AI content. 
2. **AI, the faster machine for creating monetizable garbage:** Human content is not allowed anymore, bcause it doesn't make money. If money is the reason for using the internet, let's find out the most efficient way of making money on it! Eventually, we realized that the internet doesn't have any human content anymore. So we passively decided to be online just for being "fed-content".
3. **Waiting became the trade-off of quality content.** Everything became "easier" to know. We started to wait less for an answer, believing that answer is the most accurate we could possibly find. 
**Quality is worth, you should pay for it**. If it's not with money, at least with privacy. But, wasn't the internet the only way we managed to create for publishing quality content and informational stuff for free? Just as a huge library, that also contains personal ideas, from everyone who wants to publish it...
## The algorithm, <span class="tc-color tc-color--purple" style="color: var(--color-purple)">another reason why the internet isn't reliable anymore.</span>
Now everything is almost entirely offered by big tech companies that tells us what to consume. There's no more privacy or freedom while searching the web, no matter which format (videos on Youtube, pictures on Pinterest, news on X, opinions on Reddit, code on GitHub, and the list goes on...). Also comments are ordered from "Top" or "Relevance" by default. And it makes everything feel less human-reliable. Because conversations doesn't work this way... The last voice is what we heard first... Maybe comments should be displayed by "Newest" again, no matter how many likes it have. It feels like there's no more space for expressing ourselves anymore, because you should be known first. You should be accepted and approved by others. So the internet now feels like a huge amount of people screaming out loud on a desert. 

And that's why there is no "Turn Off" button on the internet. We live always online. Always aware... always consume. There's no break, nor pause. That is exhausting and frustrating. Nowadays, everything is so catchy and turning on any screen ends up on infinite consuming. We don't know how to stop consuming. We should be aware of this and take action. Every website try it's best to make us stay forever on it. 

Now, we don't enter the internet knowing what we are looking for... Well, in some point, we actually can't enter the internet. Because we already live on it, and since some years ago we're trying to leave and don't know how.

This reality feels overwhelming bigger than us. It feels like there is no hope for surfing the web usefully and with serendipity anymore...
