The "Infinite" itself is for human beings a natural desire. An anonymous poet around the globe someday said: 
> [!note] Anonymous quote
> Humans are an instant that is willing to last forever...

The infinite as a thing many would say that doesn't exist, or at least it ain't reachable for us. That might be a great debate, but if you think about it in a more "daily-life-kinda" way, maybe we've actually reached the infinite, as a tangible thing.
## The Internet, <span class="tc-color tc-color--cyan" style="color: var(--color-cyan)">the infinite library of human knowledge.</span>
As time goes by, we tend to think more vividly that the Internet is growing wider. At this moment in time, the Internet feels overwhelmingly huge. That enormous number, for us, is just too aproximated to infinite that we reduce the internet as the infinite itself.
#### Quality content and the quantity of it.
I also think is important to distinguish which content is catalogued as "quality content" and which isn't. Also, I think is worth to the debate distinguish the matter in question. Probably, quality content for gardeners won't be the same as for developers. In addition to that, not every topic have the same quantity of content. The mainstream era has began years ago, and now almost every easy-to-find website is content-seller oriented. 
## The Internet,<span class="tc-color tc-color--cyan" style="color: var(--color-cyan)"> the infinitely useless human's library.</span>
Many people say used to be like this: A place where you could find the answer or just nothing. The internet used to be a limited place where real people just dropped their experience and their learnings for free. Or at least that's the utopia that other people describes. I've never lived that era. 

Now, the internet isn't that useful anymore, and we need to find a solution to all of this. If we want to make it useful again, or at least, manage a way to surf thorough the garbage and stumble by quality information.
