# The music I enjoy.
Here you'll find a list of all the recent albums/tracks/sounds I've been listening too. I enjoy a lot learning about the back-story of each piece. I usually dig deeper inside the creative process of the artist doing they work.

---
## The Light For Days
Jacob Collier - 2025

| ![[thelightfordays.webp\|235]] | ![[thelightfordays - 2.webp\|334]] |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
- This album is just perfect. Not because every song is good, but exactly because it have more than one "not good" song. Or at least it contains mentionable tracks that "could be better". And, with intention or not, that fact makes this album excellent. Is the "perfect with its imperfection" phrase made album. It is about being human, and humans not always have energy, time, good ideas or good songs. Something Heavy is the "Little Blue" of this album. This is an album for stay quiet for a while and rewind our time. This piece is for waiting, for staying, for making space for staying still in middle of the noise and rush of daily life.

### Love & Hyperbole 
Alessia Cara - 2025

| ![[Love & Hyperbole - Alessia Cara.webp\|243]] | ![[Love & Hyperbole - Alessia.webp\|351x180]] |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |

- This album tells a really specific story that makes it, at least for me, one of the best Pop and R&B albums of this year. I think is one of the most developed albums of her career as a musician. I've been listening to Alessia for quite a while now. And I can say that, in this new album, she nailed it. This piece have a lot of personality. It have a pretty distinguishable sound that I couldn't find in any album recently. Or at least, I couldn't find it in addition to its simplicity and "easy to listen to" kinda vibe.
